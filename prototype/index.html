What this prototype covers
  • FR-1: detect your province with GPS (coordinates go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim only when you tap Detect location), or choose it manually.
  • FR-2: pick one of 9 emergency categories on the home screen, or browse hotlines by category. See the urgency tag, clear the filter.
  • FR-3: high-urgency call screen, or low-urgency case pack.
  • FR-4: enter a natural-language emergency query in English or Thai; review the suggested category, province, and urgency; correct the category or province; inspect alternative categories or the “Not sure” emergency-call fallback when unclear.
  • FR-5 (new): live link to a trusted contact, auto-delete.
  • Language: tap the language button at the top to switch between English and Thai.
  • Concept only: "Emergency services see it" needs a partnership and is outside the current SRS.
  • AI-assisted interpretation is simulated with local keyword rules in this prototype; no LLM API is called. All data is sample data. Nothing is saved or sent.
